The Web-Based Program Monitoring and Evaluation System for the Sangguniang Kabataan Federation will be assessed through two approaches: the **ISO/IEC 25010 software quality model** and the **Technology Acceptance Model**. This will establish that the system is functionally dependable and easy to use, and that its features will deliver accurate and meaningful support to the SK Federation, the SK Barangay Officials, and the Katipunan ng Kabataan members. The data gathered will be analysed and interpreted using the following parameters:

The responses will be tallied per indicator and the weighted mean will be computed for each characteristic. The mean obtained will then be matched against the scale ranges presented above to arrive at its verbal interpretation. The ratings of the information technology experts will be used for the ISO/IEC 25010 characteristics, while the ratings of the SK officials and the Katipunan ng Kabataan members will be used for the Technology Acceptance Model.
