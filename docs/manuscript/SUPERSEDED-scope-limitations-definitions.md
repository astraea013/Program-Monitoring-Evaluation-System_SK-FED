# 1.4 Scope and Limitations

This capstone project aims to design and develop a Web-Based Program Monitoring and Evaluation System for the Sangguniang Kabataan Federation, replacing the manual handling of youth records, program information, attendance, official documents, and inventory. The system covers eight functions: youth profiling, programs and activities, calendar scheduling, attendance, activity evaluation with automatic issuance of electronic certificates, a digital repository for official documents, inventory of barangay SK properties with stock status monitoring, and dashboards with reports, notifications, and analytics. Access is governed by user role. The Administrator manages user accounts, assigns user roles including the designation of the SK Chairman of each barangay, sets system-wide configurations, and maintains the master data used by the other roles. The Administrator does not verify the registration of Katipunan ng Kabataan members. The SK Federation officials consolidate the records submitted by the barangay councils, review the programs and activities encoded by each barangay, store official documents such as minutes of meetings, resolutions, letters, and reports, and produce consolidated reports and analytics for the whole federation. The federation does not keep an inventory of its own, although it may view the inventory records submitted by the barangay councils as part of the consolidated reports. The SK Barangay Officials verify the registration of Katipunan ng Kabataan members applying from their own barangay, confirming residency and age before the account is activated. They maintain the youth profiles of their own barangay, create and encode their programs and activities, generate and upload the QR code for each activity they conduct so that attendance can be captured, monitor the attendance and evaluation results of their activities, view the records of electronic certificates issued to their participants, and submit records that the federation consolidates, with access limited to their assigned barangay. They also keep the inventory of the equipment and supplies of their own barangay, where each item carries its total quantity, the quantity already issued, the quantity still available, and a status of available, low stock, or out of stock. They encode newly acquired items, act on the borrowing requests of the youth, and record the return of borrowed equipment, and the system deducts the issued quantity and updates the status automatically whenever a request is approved. The Katipunan ng Kabataan members must register for an account and log in before they can use the system, after which they can view the calendar of programs and activities, receive announcements, view the accomplishments of the federation, scan the QR code displayed at an activity by the SK Barangay Official to record their attendance, answer the evaluation form for that same activity, receive an electronic certificate of participation generated automatically upon completion of the evaluation, and submit comments and suggestions for improvement. They may also view the equipment and supplies of their barangay together with the quantity still available, request the items they need for an activity they help organize, follow the action taken on their request, and see the items recorded under their name until these are returned. The system operates online and can be accessed through computers or mobile devices using a web browser. It is developed using Agile Scrum and is evaluated using ISO/IEC 25010 for software quality and the Technology Acceptance Model for user acceptance.

The system has several limitations. It covers only one SK Federation and does not integrate with the databases of the Department of the Interior and Local Government, the National Youth Commission, or any other national agency, so reports intended for those offices are generated by the system but submitted separately. The QR code identifies the activity and not the person, so a Katipunan ng Kabataan member must be logged in before scanning for the attendance to be recorded under their name, and youth who attend without a registered account are encoded manually by an SK officer. Registration is limited to residents aged fifteen to thirty who belong to the Katipunan ng Kabataan, and an applicant must select their barangay during registration so that the request reaches the correct SK Chairman for verification. An account remains inactive until the chairman approves it, so a member whose registration has not been acted upon cannot log in, scan for attendance, or answer an evaluation form. The system records and reports attendance, evaluation results, and program data in figures and charts, but it does not decide whether a program succeeded or failed, does not recommend courses of action, and does not predict future participation, since those judgments remain with the SK officials. The electronic certificate is issued only to members who complete the evaluation form of an activity they attended, and it certifies participation alone rather than any level of performance. The inventory module records the quantities, issuance, and status of the properties of each barangay council only, since the federation holds no inventory of its own, and a Katipunan ng Kabataan member may request items but cannot change the stock records, which only the SK Barangay Officials of that barangay can do. The module does not handle procurement, depreciation, or the budget, disbursement, or liquidation of SK funds. Performance depends on a stable internet connection and on the accuracy of the data entered by the users, since the system cannot correct information that is encoded incorrectly at the source. The system is not a mobile application, although it can be opened through a mobile browser, and it does not work offline.

# 1.5 Definition of Terms

The terms defined below explain the basic concepts and factors discussed during the conceptualization and development of the Web-Based Program Monitoring and Evaluation System for the Sangguniang Kabataan Federation, as well as the contextual information on how the researchers apply these concepts in their study.

**Activity** — a single undertaking conducted by the SK under a program, such as a seminar, sports event, clean-up drive, or training session. In this system, each activity has its own record, its own QR code, its own attendance list, and its own evaluation form.

**Administrator (Admin)** — the account with the highest level of access in the system. The Administrator manages user accounts, assigns roles including the designation of the SK Chairman of each barangay, sets system-wide configurations, and maintains the master data used by the other roles. The Administrator does not verify Katipunan ng Kabataan registrations and does not encode barangay youth profiles or activity records.

**Analytics** — the processing of stored records into figures and comparisons that the SK officials use for decisions, such as participation rates per barangay, attendance trends across activities, and average evaluation scores per program.

**Attendance Record** — the entry created when a Katipunan ng Kabataan member scans the QR code of an activity while logged in. It contains the member's identity, the activity attended, and the date and time of the scan, and it is the basis for computing eligibility for succeeding programs.

**Available Quantity** — the number of units of an inventory item that remain on hand in a barangay, computed by the system as the total quantity less the quantity already issued and not yet returned.

**Calendar Scheduling** — the module where the SK Barangay Officials set the date, time, and venue of the programs and activities they create, and where Katipunan ng Kabataan members view the schedule of upcoming activities.

**Comprehensive Barangay Youth Development Plan (CBYDP)** — the three-year plan that every Sangguniang Kabataan is required to prepare under Republic Act No. 10742. In this system, the records of programs, activities, attendance, and evaluations serve as the documentary basis for preparing and reporting this plan.

**Dashboard** — the screen that presents a summary of stored records in charts and figures upon login. Each role sees a dashboard limited to the data it is allowed to access.

**Digital Repository** — the module where official documents such as minutes of meetings, resolutions, letters, and reports are uploaded, stored, categorized, and retrieved. It replaces the practice of keeping these documents in separate applications and storage locations.

**Electronic Certificate (E-Certificate)** — the certificate of participation generated automatically by the system and released to a Katipunan ng Kabataan member once they complete the evaluation form of an activity they attended. It carries the member's name, the activity, and the date, and serves as proof of their cooperation in SK programs.

**Eligibility** — the standing of a Katipunan ng Kabataan member to join a succeeding SK program, determined by the system from the member's recorded attendance history rather than from the recollection of officials.

**Equipment Request** — the entry a Katipunan ng Kabataan member submits to borrow equipment or supplies for an activity, which the SK Barangay Officials of that barangay act upon. Once approved, the system deducts the quantity issued, records the items under the name of the requesting member, and restores the quantity when the items are returned.

**Evaluation Form** — the online questionnaire opened after attendance is recorded, through which a Katipunan ng Kabataan member rates the activity just attended. The responses are stored directly in the database and require no manual encoding.

**Inventory Management** — the module that records the equipment, supplies, and properties of each barangay youth council. Each item carries its total quantity, the quantity already issued, the quantity still available, and a stock status, so the SK Barangay Officials can see at any time how much of an item remains before acting on a request. The SK Federation does not keep an inventory of its own and only views the inventory records the barangay councils submit.

**ISO/IEC 25010** — the software product quality model used in this study to evaluate the technical quality of the developed system through the assessment of information technology experts.

**Katipunan ng Kabataan (KK)** — all residents of the barangay aged fifteen to thirty as defined under Republic Act No. 10742. In this system, KK members are the youth participants who register for an account, attend activities, answer evaluation forms, request equipment for activities they help organize, and submit comments and suggestions.

**KK Member Registration** — the required step in which a resident belonging to the Katipunan ng Kabataan creates an account and selects their barangay before gaining access to the system. The account stays inactive until the SK Chairman of the selected barangay verifies it, and without an active account a youth cannot log in, scan for attendance, or answer an evaluation form.

**Notification** — the message generated by the system to inform users of upcoming activities, announcements from the federation, and updates on records relevant to their role.

**Program** — a set of related activities organized by the SK toward a youth development objective. A program contains one or more activities and serves as the grouping used in monitoring and reporting.

**QR Code** — a two-dimensional barcode of black and white squares that stores data. In this system, the SK Barangay Official uploads and displays a QR code for a given activity, and Katipunan ng Kabataan members scan it using the camera of their mobile device to open the attendance record and proceed to the evaluation form for that same activity.

**Role-Based Access** — the control mechanism that limits each user to the functions and records that their assigned position requires. The four roles in this system are the Administrator, the SK Federation official, the SK Barangay official, and the KK member.

**Sangguniang Kabataan (SK)** — the youth council of the barangay, composed of a chairperson and kagawad elected by the Katipunan ng Kabataan, and mandated under Republic Act No. 10742 to plan, implement, and evaluate youth development programs.

**SK Barangay Chairman** — the elected head of a barangay youth council who, in this system, verifies the registration of Katipunan ng Kabataan members applying from their own barangay by confirming residency and age before the account is activated.

**SK Barangay Official** — the user role assigned to the elected officials of a barangay youth council. This role maintains the youth profiles of its own barangay, creates and encodes its programs and activities, generates and uploads the QR code for each activity it conducts, keeps the inventory of the equipment and supplies of its own barangay, acts on the borrowing requests of the youth, and submits records to the federation, with access limited to its assigned barangay.

**SK Federation** — the Pederasyon ng mga Sangguniang Kabataan at the municipal or city level, composed of the SK chairpersons of all barangays, which oversees and consolidates the work of the barangay youth councils. In this system, the federation reviews and consolidates the records encoded by each barangay rather than encoding activity records itself. This is the level of organization the system is built for.

**Stock Status** — the label the system assigns to an inventory item based on its available quantity, shown as available, low stock, or out of stock, and updated automatically whenever an approved equipment request reduces the quantity on hand or a return restores it.

**Technology Acceptance Model (TAM)** — the framework used in this study to measure whether the intended users will accept and use the system, assessed through perceived usefulness and perceived ease of use.

**Web-Based System** — an application accessed through an internet browser on a computer or mobile device rather than installed on each machine, allowing SK officials and KK members to reach the same records from different locations.

**Youth Profile** — the record of a Katipunan ng Kabataan member stored in the system, containing personal information, barangay of residence, educational or employment status, and participation history. It replaces the youth information previously recorded manually across different applications.
