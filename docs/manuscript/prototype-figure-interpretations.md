## SK FEDERATION

**Figure 20. SK Federation Dashboard**

Figure 20 showed the dashboard of the SK Federation. It opened with a federation-wide summary of the youth profiles, programs and activities, attendance, and evaluation results consolidated from all barangays. The figures came from the records encoded by the barangay councils, so the federation read what the barangays submitted rather than encoding activity records itself.

**Figure 21. SK Federation Youth Profiling**

Figure 21 showed the youth profiling page of the SK Federation. The page listed the Katipunan ng Kabataan members of every barangay with their barangay of residence and participation history, and allowed filtering by barangay. The federation viewed these records for monitoring, while the encoding and updating of profiles remained with the SK Barangay Officials.

**Figure 22. SK Federation Program and Activities**

Figure 22 showed the programs and activities page of the SK Federation. The table listed the programs encoded by each barangay together with their centre of participation, schedule, budget, and status, aligned with the Comprehensive Barangay Youth Development Plan and the Annual Barangay Youth Investment Program. The federation reviewed and consolidated these records across barangays.

**Figure 23. SK Federation Activity Calendar**

Figure 23 showed the activity calendar of the SK Federation. The scheduled programs and activities of all barangays appeared in one calendar view, so that conflicts and gaps in the youth programs of the federation could be seen in a single screen.

**Figure 24. SK Federation Participation and Eligibility**

Figure 24 showed the participation and eligibility page of the SK Federation. It presented the attendance and evaluation records of the members across barangays, from which the system computed the standing of each member for succeeding programs. Eligibility was therefore drawn from stored attendance rather than from the recollection of officials.

**Figure 25. SK Federation Budget Allocation and Approval**

Figure 25 showed the budget allocation and approval page of the SK Federation. The summary cards presented the allocation for the period, the amount already approved to the barangays, the remaining allocation, and the number of barangays reporting. Under the Pending Proposals tab, each proposal submitted by a barangay appeared with its proposed amount, the programs it covered, the official who submitted it, and the documents attached to it, with an Approve and a Return button. An approved proposal was deducted from the allocation of the federation, while a returned proposal was sent back to the council with remarks for revision.

**Figure 26. SK Federation Reports and Analytics**

Figure 26 showed the reports and analytics page of the SK Federation. The page turned the consolidated records into figures and charts covering participation rates per barangay, attendance trends, and evaluation averages, and the reports produced here could be exported or printed for submission.

**Figure 27. SK Federation Document Repository**

Figure 27 showed the document repository of the SK Federation. Minutes of meetings, resolutions, letters, and reports were uploaded, categorized, and retrieved in one place, replacing the practice of keeping these documents in separate applications and storage locations.

**Figure 28. SK Federation KK Member Content**

Figure 28 showed the KK member content page of the SK Federation. The federation posted announcements and published accomplishments for the members to read, and replied to the comments and suggestions the members submitted.

## SK BARANGAY OFFICIAL

**Figure 29. SK Barangay Official Dashboard**

Figure 29 showed the dashboard of the SK Barangay Official. It summarized the records of one barangay only, covering its youth profiles, programs and activities, attendance, evaluation results, inventory, and budget, since the access of this role was limited to its assigned barangay.

**Figure 30. SK Barangay Official Member Verification**

Figure 30 showed the member verification page of the SK Barangay Official. The registrations of applicants who selected this barangay appeared as a pending list, and the official confirmed the residency and age of each applicant before activating the account. The page established that the designated SK Chairman of the barangay verified the registrations rather than the Administrator.

**Figure 31. SK Barangay Official Youth Profiling**

Figure 31 showed the youth profiling page of the SK Barangay Official. The official encoded and maintained the records of the Katipunan ng Kabataan members of the barangay, containing their personal information, educational or employment status, and participation history, replacing the youth information previously recorded manually across different applications.

**Figure 32. SK Barangay Official Programs and Activities**

Figure 32 showed the programs and activities page of the SK Barangay Official. The table listed the programs of the barangay with their code, centre of participation, schedule, budget, and budget status, and the panel on the right presented the plan alignment, the allocated, utilised, and balance amounts, the completion status of the budget with a note on the remaining amount, and the target against the number of youth reached.

**Figure 33. SK Barangay Official Activity Calendar**

Figure 33 showed the activity calendar of the SK Barangay Official. The official set the date, time, and venue of the programs and activities created for the barangay, and the schedule appeared here and on the calendar of the members.

**Figure 34. SK Barangay Official QR Attendance**

Figure 34 showed the QR attendance page of the SK Barangay Official. The system generated a unique QR code for each activity, which the official displayed or printed at the venue for the members to scan. The attendance recorded through the scans appeared on the same page, and youth who attended without a registered account were encoded manually.

**Figure 35. SK Barangay Official Participation and Eligibility**

Figure 35 showed the participation and eligibility page of the SK Barangay Official. It presented the attendance and evaluation records of the members of the barangay, from which the system determined their standing for succeeding programs.

**Figure 36. SK Barangay Official Inventory**

Figure 36 showed the inventory page of the SK Barangay Official. Each item carried its total quantity, the quantity already issued, the quantity still available, and a stock status, and the equipment requests filed by the members of the barangay appeared as a pending list for the official to act upon. When a request was approved, the system deducted the issued quantity and updated the stock status automatically.

**Figure 37. SK Barangay Official Budget**

Figure 37 showed the budget page of the SK Barangay Official. The summary cards presented the proposed amount, the amount approved by the federation, the amount utilised, and the balance. The Proposals tab listed the budget proposals of the council in their three states, namely pending, approved with the remarks of the federation, and returned for revision with a button to revise and resubmit. The table of programme budgets marked each program as complete or incomplete with a note explaining the remaining amount, and the page also produced the monthly and annual reports that compared the approved budget against the actual spending for the period. A notice on the page stated that budget records were open to the SK officials and the federation only and that the members had no access to them.

**Figure 38. SK Barangay Official Document Repository**

Figure 38 showed the document repository of the SK Barangay Official. The official uploaded and retrieved the official documents of the barangay, including the supporting documents attached to the budget proposals submitted to the federation.

**Figure 39. SK Barangay KK Member Content**

Figure 39 showed the KK member content page of the SK Barangay Official. The official posted announcements for the members of the barangay and replied to the comments and suggestions they submitted.

**Figure 40. SK Barangay Official Reports and Analytics**

Figure 40 showed the reports and analytics page of the SK Barangay Official. The stored records of the barangay were presented as figures and charts covering attendance, evaluation results, program status, and budget utilisation, and the reports generated here were exported or printed and submitted to the federation.

## KK MEMBER

**Figure 41. KK Member Homepage**

Figure 41 showed the homepage of the KK Member. After logging in with a verified account, the member saw the announcements, the upcoming activities, and the accomplishments of the federation. The navigation showed no budget page, since budget records were not accessible to the members.

**Figure 42. KK Member Calendar**

Figure 42 showed the calendar of the KK Member. The scheduled programs and activities of the barangay appeared with their date, time, and venue, so that the member could see which activities were upcoming.

**Figure 43. KK Member Attendance**

Figure 43 showed the attendance page of the KK Member. The activities the member attended were listed with the date and time of each scan, and this history served as the basis for the eligibility of the member to join succeeding programs.

**Figure 44. KK Member Evaluations**

Figure 44 showed the evaluations page of the KK Member. The evaluations already submitted were listed with the ratings the member gave, together with the answers recorded for each question, while an activity attended but not yet evaluated appeared with its form still open. The page stated that the electronic certificate was released only once the evaluation was submitted.

**Figure 45. KK Member Certificates**

Figure 45 showed the certificates page of the KK Member. The electronic certificates generated automatically upon completion of each evaluation were listed with the activity and the date, and each certificate served as proof of the cooperation of the member in the programs of the SK.

**Figure 46. KK Member Request Equipment**

Figure 46 showed the equipment request page of the KK Member. The member viewed the items of the barangay inventory with the quantity still available, submitted a request for the items needed for an activity, and followed the action taken on that request by the SK Barangay Official.

**Figure 47. KK Member Reports and Documents**

Figure 47 showed the reports and documents page of the KK Member. The accomplishment reports and the documents published by the barangay and the federation were made available for the members to read, which supported the public disclosure required of the Sangguniang Kabataan.
